MSc Project · Data Science · Business Intelligence

An Intelligent Data Analytics and Machine Learning Framework for Predictive Business Decision-Making

Case study: 500,000 sales transactions of Dan Cake products in Bangladesh, January 2020 – September 2026. The framework answers three management questions: what happened, why it happened, and what is likely to happen next.
